# Activity: Hold the Iteration Retrospective

**Activity ID**: 557
**Order**: 5
**Phase**: Construction
**Dependencies**: Predecessor: Activity 556 (Execute the Iteration)
Successor: Activity 558 (Raise Iteration Improvement Proposals)

## Description

Hold the Iteration Retrospective

## Guidance

## Objective

Convert the iteration's delivery and assessment evidence into owned learning without rewriting history.

## Required inputs

- Iteration Frame and accepted PIN/MIN records.
- Release/acceptance evidence.
- Latest complete Iteration Assessment Report and Project-Management Tickets.
- Charter and participants named in its Team Roster.

## Procedure

1. Create `docs/project/retros/<iteration_id>.md`.
2. Compare planned versus actual outcome, duration, throughput, quality, coordination, and assessment signals.
3. For each material variance, record evidence, causal hypothesis, impact, and whether it is:
   - a product/code/delivery concern to track as backlog or Risk/Problem ticket; or
   - a repeatable process concern eligible for an IIP.
4. Record decisions, action owners, due dates, and experiments. Do not turn every complaint into a process change.
5. Reassess team maturity only when the iteration produced relevant evidence; update the Charter with evidence and date, not intuition.

## Completion evidence

The retrospective cites source records, separates fact from interpretation, classifies each concern, assigns every accepted action, records unchanged/open questions, and has participant acknowledgment.

## Agent

**Name**: Delivery Manager (DM)
**Description**: Single merged PM/DM role. Staffed at Kickoff. Owns the Charter, is the recipient of all Risk/Problem tickets raised by Assess the Iteration, presides over cadence meetings, and determines iteration cadence/length.
